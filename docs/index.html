Where to put the files

Pick your device. Spotify only sees local files in the places below.

Windows PC

  1. Save the songs in one folder you'll keep. Good choices: C:\Users\<you>\Music or C:\Users\<you>\Downloads. Spotify already looks in both. If your browser asks where to save, pick that folder.
  2. Open Spotify and click your profile picture → Settings.
  3. Find Your Library (or Local files) and turn on Show Local Files.
  4. If your folder isn't Music or Downloads, click Add a source and choose it.
  5. Your songs appear under Your Library → Local Files. Add them to a playlist.

Mac

  1. Save the songs in one folder you'll keep. Good choices: ~/Music or ~/Downloads (Finder → Go → Home). Spotify already looks in both.
  2. Open Spotify and choose Spotify → Settings from the menu bar (or click your profile picture → Settings).
  3. Under Your Library (or Local files) turn on Show Local Files.
  4. If your folder is somewhere else, click Add a source and choose it. If macOS asks whether Spotify can access that folder, click Allow.
  5. Your songs appear under Your Library → Local Files. Add them to a playlist.

Phone

  1. Save the songs in the phone's Music or Downloads folder. When your browser downloads them they land in Downloads; you can move them to Music with the Files app.
  2. Open Spotify → Home → Settings (gear icon) → Local files.
  3. Turn on Show audio files from this device. Allow access to your music/files if asked.
  4. Your songs appear under Your Library → Liked Songs / Local Files.

Heads up: Spotify on iPhone can't read songs saved on the phone itself. It only gets local files from a playlist on your PC or Mac.

  1. On your PC or Mac, add the local files to a playlist (set them up using the PC or Mac steps first).
  2. Put the iPhone on the same Wi-Fi as that computer, signed in to the same Spotify account (Premium is needed to download).
  3. On the iPhone open Spotify → Settings → Local Files and turn on Show Local Files.
  4. Open the playlist and turn on Download. Keep both devices on and nearby until it finishes.

Sending songs to the iPhone from this page still works - they're saved to the Files app (Downloads). Useful as a backup, but Spotify won't play them from there.

Before you send: run the songs through Fix local files in the toolkit first, and send the fixed copies. Spotify matches local tracks by file name and length, so every device should get the same fixed file.
Files go directly between your two devices over an encrypted connection. A public relay is only used to introduce them to each other; it never sees your songs. Keep this page open on both devices until the transfer finishes.
