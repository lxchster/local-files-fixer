Where do the files go?

Pick the device you downloaded them to. Spotify only sees local files in the places below.

Windows PC
  1. Keep the songs in one folder you won't move. Good choices: C:\Users\<you>\Music or C:\Users\<you>\Downloads. Spotify already looks in both.
  2. Open Spotify, click your profile picture → Settings.
  3. Under Your Library (or Local files) turn on Show Local Files.
  4. If your folder isn't Music or Downloads, click Add a source and choose it.
  5. The songs appear under Your Library → Local Files. Add them to a playlist.
Mac
  1. Keep the songs in one folder you won't move. Good choices: ~/Music or ~/Downloads (Finder → Go → Home). Spotify already looks in both.
  2. Open Spotify and choose Spotify → Settings from the menu bar.
  3. Under Your Library (or Local files) turn on Show Local Files.
  4. If your folder is somewhere else, click Add a source and choose it. If macOS asks whether Spotify can access it, click Allow.
  5. The songs appear under Your Library → Local Files. Add them to a playlist.
Phone
  1. Put the songs in the phone's Music or Downloads folder. Downloads from your browser land in Downloads; move them to Music with the Files app if you like.
  2. Open Spotify → Home → Settings (gear icon) → Local files.
  3. Turn on Show audio files from this device, and allow access to your music/files if asked.
  4. The songs appear under Your Library as local files.

Heads up: Spotify on iPhone can't read songs saved on the phone itself. It only gets local files from a playlist on your PC or Mac.

  1. On your PC or Mac, add the local files to a playlist (use the PC or Mac steps first).
  2. Put the iPhone on the same Wi-Fi as that computer, signed in to the same Spotify account (Premium is needed to download).
  3. On the iPhone open Spotify → Settings → Local Files and turn on Show Local Files.
  4. Open the playlist and turn on Download. Keep both devices on and nearby until it finishes.

Songs you download to the iPhone from this page are saved in the Files app, which is handy as a backup, but Spotify won't play them from there.

Tip: run the songs through Fix local files before you send them, so every device gets the same fixed file.
